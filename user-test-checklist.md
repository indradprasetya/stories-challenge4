# User Testing Checklist

Please play at least two chapters and check:

- Is it clear which action to drag and where to drop it?
- Do the character expressions and speech bubbles make sense after each choice?
- Are the green, yellow, orange, and red face indicators understandable?
- Does unfinished gameplay resume correctly after leaving and reopening a chapter?
- After completion, are the stars, summary, and tip clear?
- Did you find any confusing action, missing image, wrong text, lag, or accidental drop?
- Which chapter felt too easy, too difficult, or unfair?
