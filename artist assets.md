# Artist Assets

This catalog is the source of truth for drawings shared across all six chapters. Reuse an asset only when its emotion, pose, included objects, and viewing angle match.

The character workload is intentionally limited to **19 general expressions** and **7 unique scene poses**. Important objects such as crayons, paper, scrapes, and bandages are baked into the unique poses, so the app does not need prop positioning or overlay logic.
